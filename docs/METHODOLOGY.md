# Methodology

The simulation advances in one-hour increments. A dust-perturbed lunar
daylight curve supplies power. Available energy above a battery reserve limits
the mass that can be excavated, crushed and hauled during each hour.

Specific delivered-mass energy combines three transparent components:

1. excavation energy adjusted for compaction and slope;
2. crushing energy per kilogram; and
3. round-trip rover energy divided by payload mass.

Temperature phase and accumulated abrasive wear derate throughput. Seeded
boulder events pause production, while reaching the wear limit triggers a
maintenance hour and partial wear reset. Tests enforce mass conservation,
reproducibility, energy bounds, and expected comparative statics.
